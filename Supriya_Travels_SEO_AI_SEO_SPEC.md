# Supriya Travels of India --- SEO & AI Search Optimization Specification

**Document purpose:** Master SEO specification for the Supriya Travels
of India website.

**Primary objective:** Build strong organic visibility for Hajj, Umrah,
domestic tours, international tours, tourist visas and air-ticketing
services, while making the company's entity, services, destinations and
contact information easy for conventional search engines and AI-powered
search/recommendation systems to understand.

> **Important:** SEO and AI-search visibility cannot be guaranteed. The
> implementation should maximize crawlability, indexability, relevance,
> entity clarity, content quality, local signals and citation/discovery
> opportunities. Google states that the same foundational SEO practices
> remain relevant for AI features; OpenAI states that allowing
> OAI-SearchBot to crawl a site helps make public content available to
> ChatGPT Search, but there is no guarantee of top placement. Bing
> likewise connects SEO fundamentals with eligibility for AI grounding
> and citations.
